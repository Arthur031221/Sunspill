# Code of conduct

Sunspill is for people who want to know where the sun lands in a room. Everyone is welcome to use it, report problems and send changes, whatever their age, background, language or experience.

What we ask:

- Be kind and assume good faith. Say what you mean in plain words.
- Criticise the code, the text or the idea, never the person.
- Do not harass, insult or threaten anyone, and do not share somebody's private details, including where they live. Rooms and places in issues are shared by choice and are not to be passed on.
- Help people who are new to the project.

Comments that break these rules can be edited or removed, and people who keep breaking them can be blocked from the project. To report something, use the contact on the maintainer's GitHub profile, or open an issue that does not name anyone and ask for a private way to talk.
